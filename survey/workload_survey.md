# Employee Workload & Wellbeing Survey

*Prepared by the HR Analytics team · Anonymous · About 5 minutes*

> **Portfolio note:** This is a draft written for a practice project using fictional data. It has not been sent to any employees.

## Why we are asking

We want to understand how your workload feels day to day, so we can make work better for you and your team. There are no right or wrong answers. Your honest view is what helps most.

**Before you start**

- This survey is **anonymous**. Please do not write your name or any detail that could identify you.
- It is voluntary. You can skip any question.
- Results are reported only for groups of 10 or more people, so nobody can be singled out.
- Your answers will not be used in performance reviews.

## Notes for HR before sending (remove this section before sharing with employees)

- **Confirm the promises** in "Before you start" (anonymity, no use in reviews) with leadership. Breaking them destroys trust, and people stop answering honestly.
- **Protect small groups.** Report only groups of 10 or more. In a small team, drop the department question.
- **Pilot first.** Ask 5 employees to complete it and fix any unclear wording.
- **Decide who acts** on the results, and tell employees afterwards what changed.
- **Compare at group level.** Because the survey is anonymous, you cannot link answers to individual employees. Compare survey results by department and tenure band with the attrition dashboard instead.
- **Know its limit.** The survey reaches current employees only. It cannot explain why people who already left went; exit interviews would be needed for that.
- **This is an AI-assisted draft.** A person in HR must review and adapt it before it goes out.
